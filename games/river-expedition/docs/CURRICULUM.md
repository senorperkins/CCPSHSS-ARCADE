# River Expedition: curriculum alignment — 0.5

All 40 items are original four-option practice, not copied or official test questions. Questions about game controls, driftwood damage, and fictional-stop status have been removed. Sets remain five correct answers each. Camp questions draw on the preceding Field Note; docking questions also draw on prior classroom knowledge.

The 2023 standards text was checked in the adopted April 2023 VDOE document, pp. 21–22, hosted by a school: https://wischools.org/wp-content/uploads/History-and-SS-Standards.pdf . The current VDOE landing page was accessible to search, but its direct downloads returned 403. Later clarifications and full assessment-blueprint coverage are not certified.

Public VDOE Spring 2014 Virginia Studies items 1–3 and 15–18 were visually reviewed for formats: short content questions, evidence, regional characteristics, and river/location relationships. Those items assess the older 2008 standards; their numbering is NOT used as current SOL numbering. Original document: https://www.doe.virginia.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/20875/638043643497370000 ; accessible mirror: https://www.solpass.org/released-online/ss4-2013/4VAstudiesSOL2014.pdf . This game practices single-select items only, not the full range of SOL technology-enhanced formats.

Scope: selected VS.1b/c, VS.3a/b/d/e, and VS.4a content. Jamestown and the supply stop focus on Tidewater history and trade. Richmond and camp focus on the Fall Line and Piedmont transition, with a short cumulative review. Fictional stops do not acquire invented historical claims. Regional alignment means the region visited in the game, not a separate regional SOL test.

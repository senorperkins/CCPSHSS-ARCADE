Do not treat this release as validation of blueprint weighting or complete coverage.
- [2023 VS.1 study guide](https://www.solpass.org/z-site/va/2023Standards/vs1-2023.html): secondary cross-check of VS.1b/c geography, city/river relationships, and Fall Line. Exact current VDOE Word standards also returned access denied, so teacher verification of the final alignment is outstanding.
- [National Park Service: Richmond's Indigenous heritage](https://home.nps.gov/tripideas/richmond-s-indigenous-heritage.htm) and [Virginia DWR: James River through Richmond](https://dwr.virginia.gov/wp-content/uploads/2011-James-River-Through-Richmond.pdf): Richmond's Fall Line and non-tidal/tidal transition.
- State outlines extracted from [PublicaMundi MappingAPI US state GeoJSON](https://github.com/PublicaMundi/MappingAPI/blob/master/data/geojson/us-states.json), a generalized state-boundary example dataset. Retain attribution when reusing it. River vertices are original, hand-generalized geographic waypoints; widths are exaggerated and small meanders omitted.

Learning objectives: navigate connected waterways; associate the four named rivers with their cities; explain the Chesapeake connection; experience the Coastal Plain/Piedmont navigation barrier. The upstream cargo scenario is a teaching abstraction, not a reconstruction of a specific historical voyage. Carrying cargo represents transfer around rapids, not a claim that all craft or modern navigation are impossible.

All source references are development documentation only. The game makes no requests to them.
